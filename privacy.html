Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

  • Who uses it. Only the owner, Mykhailo Belkov, with his own Google and Spotify accounts.
  • What it reads. Read-only: YouTube subscriptions and liked videos; Spotify top artists and tracks, recently played tracks, and followed artists.
  • What it does not do. It does not post, like, delete, or change any data. It does not read email, files, or contacts.
  • Storage. Data and OAuth tokens are kept on the owner's private server, with access limited to the owner. They are not published.
  • Sharing. Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising.
  • Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removal. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored data is then deleted.
